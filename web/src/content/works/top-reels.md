The posts that travelled furthest were the ones that felt native to the platform: short videos, carousels and retail humour that people wanted to send to a friend.

<div class="case-stats"><div><b>50K–100K+</b><span>views on top posts</span></div><div><b>3</b><span>formats that worked</span></div></div>

## The brief

Get people who are not in the trade to stop scrolling on a paint brand's post, and share it.

## What I did

- Spotted trends early and adapted them to paint and painting
- Wrote, shot, directed and edited the videos
- Tested formats and doubled down on what people shared

## Result

Top posts reached 50,000 to 100,000+ views.
