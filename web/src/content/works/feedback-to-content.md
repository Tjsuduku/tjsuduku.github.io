When a product launch drew critical comments on social media, I answered them openly and turned what people said into the next piece of marketing.

<div class="case-stats"><div><b>Public</b><span>answered in the open</span></div><div><b>Fast</b><span>replies on the same channel</span></div><div><b>1</b><span>problem turned into a post</span></div></div>

## The brief

A new product launch drew critical comments. Deleting or ignoring them would have made it worse.

## What I did

- Replied to critical comments quickly and honestly, protecting the brand without hiding the feedback
- Took the questions people kept asking and built content that answered them
- Published it on the same channels where the feedback started

## Result

Negative feedback became positive marketing content instead of a problem to bury.
