A buy-Canadian campaign I came up with and ran. Instead of talking about paint, Cloverdale used its website and social channels to feature other Canadian businesses, large and small, at a moment when people wanted to support Canadian companies.

<div class="case-stats"><div><b>Own idea</b><span>concept to launch</span></div><div><b>Beyond paint</b><span>reached new audiences</span></div><div><b>Web + social</b><span>channels</span></div></div>

## The brief

Cloverdale is a Canadian manufacturer. The challenge was to say that in a way people would share, not just scroll past.

## What I did

- Wrote the concept: Canadian businesses telling the story, Cloverdale as the host
- Featured Canadian businesses on Cloverdale's website and social channels
- Produced the posts and web content

## Result

The campaign reached audiences well outside the paint industry and led to 30 Days of Made in Canada.
