A month-long series I developed on my own: every day in November, Cloverdale features one Canadian business across its web and social channels. Each partner gets visibility, and in return engages with and shares Cloverdale's content.

<div class="case-stats"><div><b>30</b><span>Canadian businesses</span></div><div><b>30 days</b><span>one feature a day</span></div><div><b>2-way</b><span>partners share back</span></div></div>

## The brief

Build on Canadian by Canadian with a format that keeps showing up every day for a month, and time it for when support for Canadian businesses was strongest.

## What I did

- Came up with the series and the give-and-get with partners
- Contacted 30 small and large Canadian businesses one by one
- Planned the calendar, wrote and produced each feature, and tagged every partner
- Ran the series across the website and social channels

## Result

30 Canadian businesses featured through November, reaching audiences outside the paint industry.

<blockquote class="case-quote">He timed it for a moment when support for Canadian businesses resonated strongly, and it reflects the kind of thinking I rarely see this early in a career.<cite>Kevin Skelly, Marketing Manager, Cloverdale Paint</cite></blockquote>
