Cloverdale Paint launched its Colour of the Year 2027 with colour edit booths: a space where interior designers bring their clients and choose colours together, with the new colour at the centre. I led the creative and the on-site execution, working directly with the president and CEO.

<div class="case-stats"><div><b>1,000+</b><span>attendees in Vancouver</span></div><div><b>2,500+</b><span>across Vancouver and Toronto</span></div><div><b>1</b><span>creative lead, start to finish</span></div></div>

## The brief

Introduce the new colour to the people who specify paint for a living, designers, in a way that feels like a design event rather than a store promotion, and give them a reason to bring their clients.

## What I did

- Led the creative direction for the booths and the launch
- Set up and managed registration on Eventbrite
- Ran the booth on the day: designers and clients picked palettes together, with the Colour of the Year as the anchor
- Worked directly with the president and CEO on the launch

## Result

More than 1,000 people attended in Vancouver. The same format then went to Toronto, for 2,500+ attendees across both launches.
