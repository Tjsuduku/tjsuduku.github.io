I run Cloverdale's organic social. I moved the content toward original, trend-based video, carousels and meme-style retail content, planned on a steady calendar.

## The brief

Make a paint brand feel current to homeowners and small painters, not only to contractors.

## What I did

- Set the strategy: video over static, with a weekly mix of colour edit videos, static posts and meme content
- Planned weekly and monthly content calendars
- Shot, edited and published the content, and managed comments and messages

## Result

Instagram and Facebook audiences grew 348%, and the best posts reached 50,000 to 100,000+ views.
