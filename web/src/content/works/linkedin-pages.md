Cloverdale speaks to three different professional audiences on LinkedIn. I manage all three pages, each with its own voice.

## The pages

- Industrial
- Corporate
- Specifier and designer

## The brief

One brand, three professional audiences: industrial customers, the corporate audience, and the specifiers and designers who choose colours for projects.

## What I did

- Planned and published content for each audience
- Kept tone and topics separate so each page stays useful to its followers

## Result

About 12,000 combined followers across the three pages.
