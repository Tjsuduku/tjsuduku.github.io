Beyond its 82 stores, Cloverdale sells through authorized dealers across Canada. I plan and run Meta campaigns that send nearby customers to those dealers.

<div class="case-stats"><div><b>~$2,000</b><span>typical campaign budget</span></div><div><b>3</b><span>targeting layers: location, age, job title</span></div><div><b>Canada-wide</b><span>authorized dealers</span></div></div>

## The brief

Drive awareness and traffic for each dealer on a small budget, and keep cost per click low.

## What I did

- Set up geofenced audiences around each dealer, layered with age and job title
- Built creative and ran A/B tests
- Set up retargeting and optimized for CPC and CPM
- Pulled the reports and redesigned how results are shown to the team

## Result

Dealer campaigns running across Canada on budgets around $2,000, tuned for traffic and awareness.
