I use AI to produce content faster, not to replace the idea. A brief goes in, and the tools help with the drafts, the visuals and the sound, while I direct and edit the result.

## Where it helps

- Scripts, captions and campaign ideas with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
- Image and video generation for social and paid creative
- Voiceover and sound for short-form video
- Agentic workflows that take a brief to a finished, on-brand asset

## This site

The 3D head on this page was made from my own photos with Higgsfield and Meshy, and the site itself was built with Claude Code on top of an open-source 3D resume.

## Tools

ChatGPT · Claude · Claude Code · Gemini · Higgsfield · ElevenLabs · Canva · Adobe Creative Suite
