I look after Cloverdale's online reputation for all 82 store locations. Every week that means 80 to 150 Google reviews, and behind some of them a customer who needs a real answer.

<div class="case-stats"><div><b>82</b><span>store locations</span></div><div><b>80–150</b><span>reviews a week</span></div><div><b>0-star</b><span>reviews resolved too</span></div></div>

## What I did

- Answer reviews across every location, in the brand's voice
- Take complaints offline and work them through with the store team and HR
- Follow up with the customer until it is resolved, including zero-star reviews

## Result

80 to 150 reviews handled every week, and unhappy customers followed up until their issue is resolved.
